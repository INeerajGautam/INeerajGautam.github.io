01 · About

An economist who works where ecology meets livelihoods.

I'm an environmental economist passionate about climate change and sustainability, working at the point where nature, communities and economics meet.

Over seven years I've worked extensively across watershed and wetland restoration, forest bioeconomy, livelihoods and climate finance — with hands-on experience in project management and coordination. My work has spanned Payment for Ecosystem Services design, ecosystem-service valuation, and building incentive models that make conservation pay for the people living closest to the land.

I began with an engineering foundation, then moved into conservation management, and I've since carried that blend of analytical rigour and on-the-ground pragmatism from the forests of Madhya Pradesh to catchment restoration work in Rwanda.
